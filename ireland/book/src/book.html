Sunday morning — Mass for Grandad in Loughrea first, the whole clan in one pew. Then home to change into the good clothes.
Galway Races Family Day: hats on, best shirts, first race at two o'clock. There were pony rides and face paint, and serious study of the racecard by people who cannot yet read.

Birr Castle & the Great Telescope

MON 3 AUGUST · BANK HOLIDAY
A bank-holiday outing with Uncle Martin off work: castle gardens to run in, a science centre to press every button in, and the Leviathan of Parsonstown — for seventy years the biggest telescope on Earth, and still enormous.
SLOT 3AThe Great Telescope — kids for scale
The Leviathan: built 1845, mirror six feet across.
MON 3 AUG — Picnic in the gardens, home by four for farm downtime.
