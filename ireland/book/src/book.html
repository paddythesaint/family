Getting There

FRI 31 JUL → SAT 1 AUG
Bags packed, boosters inflated, and a night flight out of Dulles. By breakfast time the Ryans were blinking in Dublin Airport, and by lunch the wheels were crunching up the lane at Cahertinna — with cousins already waiting at the gate.
SAT 1 AUG — Landed 8:55am. Two hours west, one shop-run, and straight into the yard.
day 1 · Martin's birthday
THAT EVENING — No easing in: it was Uncle Martin's birthday the very day we landed — and not just any birthday: his fortieth. Cake, candles and the first pints of the week, in Martin and Leona's garden at Ballymore.

Between the big days out there were slow mornings, wellies by the door, bread in the oven and a yard full of places to hide. The kids were up at nine and out the door by five past.

A Day at the Races

SUN 2 AUGUST · BALLYBRIT
Sunday morning — Mass for Grandad in Loughrea first, the whole clan in one pew. Then home to change into the good clothes.
Galway Races Family Day: hats on, best shirts, first race at two o'clock. There were pony rides and face paint, and serious study of the racecard by people who cannot yet read.
SUN 2 AUG — Left at quarter to one; home for a very late dinner.
day 2 photos
THE BIG WIN — Evelyn studied the card, backed her horse, and collected thirty euro at the window. She has not stopped mentioning it.

Birr Castle & the Great Telescope

MON 3 AUGUST · BANK HOLIDAY
A bank-holiday outing with Uncle Martin off work: castle gardens to run in, a science centre to press every button in, and the Leviathan of Parsonstown — for seventy years the biggest telescope on Earth, and still enormous. Then a call in to Auntie Mary — Birr's own — to finish the day properly.
The Leviathan: built 1845, mirror six feet across.
MON 3 AUG — Picnic in the gardens, home by four for farm downtime.

Wildlands

TUE 4 AUGUST · MOYCULLEN
Back by popular demand — the kids loved it last year. Treetop nets strung through the sky, climbing walls, slides, and serious digging. Lunch at the café, everyone home muddy and delighted.
TUE 4 AUG — An hour west past Galway; home mid-afternoon, gloriously worn out.

Mam's Home — Into the Burren

WED 5 AUGUST · SHANNON → AILLWEE
Down to Shannon to collect Mam off the London plane — the best hug of the week — then on into the grey limestone hills of the Burren: deep under the mountain at Aillwee Caves, and out blinking into the daylight for the birds of prey.
WED 5 AUG — EI 381 into Shannon at 11:20; underground by early afternoon.
day 5 · the hawk
AILLWEE BIRDS OF PREY — A Harris's hawk swept the crowd and landed straight onto Michael's glove, cool as you like. A kite circled overhead too — one for the family bird list.
day 5 · Flaggy Shore
And on the way home…

Flaggy Shore

The limestone shelf at New Quay, where the Burren walks straight into the Atlantic. Rockpools, wave-watching, cold toes — and more cousins: Martin and Alice came out to meet us on the shore.
NEW QUAY, CO. CLARE — Shells picked with Alice: hundreds. Shells that made it home: six.

Town & a Barbecue

THU 6 AUGUST · GALWAY → LURGAN
A morning's shopping in Galway town: new clothes all round, a stack of books for the girls, and a couple of Lego sets for Michael (essential engineering supplies). Then out to Uncle Michael and Auntie Aisling's in Lurgan, where the barbecue was lit and the whole crowd gathered.
THU 6 AUG — Bags carried home; Lego construction began immediately.

Over to Francis & Kate's

FRI 7 AUGUST · BRIDGEFIELD
Last full day, and the rounds complete: a day at Francis and Kate's at Bridgefield — which meant the week had called into every single house. Goats to feed, cousins loose in the grass, one big photo to wrangle, and the suitcases waiting at home.
FRI 7 AUG — Four houses in seven days: Cahertinna, Ballymore, Lurgan, Bridgefield. Full house.
